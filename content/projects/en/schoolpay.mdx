# SchoolPay

> Side project: payment module for a web-based school management platform.

---

## 💡 Overview

Implemented the payment system for a web-based school management platform, enabling secure tuition and fee processing via **Midtrans**.

---

## ✨ Key Contributions

- Implemented backend logic to **restrict exam card access** until tuition payments were confirmed.
- Integrated various payment methods including **virtual accounts**, **e-wallets**, and **credit cards** through the Midtrans API.
- Ensured proper handling of **transaction callbacks**, status validation, and **real-time updates** to student records.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **PHP & CodeIgniter** | Backend
- **MySQL** | Database
- **JavaScript** | Client-side logic
- **Midtrans API** | Payment processing

---

## 🧭 Impact

Delivered secure, automated tuition processing that reduced manual effort and prevented access to exam cards until payment was confirmed.
