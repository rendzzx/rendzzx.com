# HRIS 7 — Human Resource Information System (Ministry of Trade)

> A centralized internal system for employee services and HR management at the **Ministry of Trade of the Republic of Indonesia**.

---

## 💡 Overview

HRIS (Human Resource Information System) is a comprehensive internal web application designed to manage employee-related administrative processes, including promotion, pension, and internal helpdesk support. The system ensures efficiency, transparency, and accuracy in handling HR workflows for civil servants.

---

## ✨ Key Contributions

- **Developed** internal service systems within the Helpdesk module to process **pension applications** based on employee rank and position.
- **Automated** the **promotion workflow**, enabling submissions to trigger automatically once eligibility criteria were fulfilled.
- **Provided guidance and technical solutions** for various web development initiatives within the HR directorate.
- **Ensured** successful design, implementation, and optimization of internal applications.
- **Offered strategic advice** on system architecture, testing, and scalability.
- **Maintained servers, APIs, and databases** (SQL Server & MySQL) for consistent performance and security.
- **Troubleshot and resolved** complex technical issues to ensure system reliability.

---

## 🧩 Tech Stack

- **PHP** | Backend
- **CodeIgniter** | Framework
- **SQL Server** | Data storage and management
- **jQuery & Bootstrap** | Frontend interactivity and responsive UI
- **JavaScript** | Workflow automation and client-side logic

---

## 🧭 Impact

The HRIS 7 system has significantly improved the efficiency of administrative operations at the Ministry of Trade by reducing manual processing times, enhancing data accuracy, and providing real-time tracking for employee service requests.
